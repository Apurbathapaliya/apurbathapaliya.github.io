Springer Nature · ESCS'26 · July 2026

Weekday Stratified Clinical Patient Appointment No-Show Analysis Using Machine Learning

E. Tokgoz, A. D. Thapaliya and N. Rimal

Missed appointments are a constant problem for clinics. They leave scheduled time unused and delay care for the patients who need it.

This paper uses machine learning to study clinical appointment no-shows, looking at the data separately for each day of the week to see whether the patterns behind missed appointments change from one weekday to the next.
